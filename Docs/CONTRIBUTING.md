At present, CMOS is a hobbyist project based on the x86_64 architecture, written in C and Assembly, and developed from scratch by a single person. However, the project requires regular updates, which a single person is unable to provide within a relatively short timeframe, so contributors are sought.
## Requirements
- C or Assembly knowledge
- interest in OSDev
- Linux or Docker environment
 
---
## Plans for future
In the future, CMOS won’t just focus on C. We also have plans to include languages such as C++ and Rust, but to lay the foundations for what I hope will be a future generation of C++ and Rust programmers, we need a well-structured CMOS for C.
